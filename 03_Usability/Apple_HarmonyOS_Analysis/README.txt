APPLE INTELLIGENCE & HARMONYOS USABILITY ANALYSIS (Sections 4.3 and 5.2)
==========================================================================

WHAT THIS FOLDER CONTAINS
--------------------------
notebooks/Reproduce_Usability_Results.ipynb
    START HERE. Recomputes every Apple/HarmonyOS usability number in the paper
    (Tables 2 and 3, the human-validation statistics, the superseded model-only
    estimate, and all sensitivity analyses) from the released files alone.
    No API calls, no raw text needed; runs in about a minute in Google Colab.
    Set BASE to this folder's path in the first cell.

notebooks/Apple_HarmonyOS_Usability_v4.ipynb
    The full collection and classification pipeline as run for the paper
    (official APIs -> Claude Sonnet 5 extraction -> stricter verification pass ->
    rule audit -> clustering -> cluster-robust statistics). Outputs were cleared
    because they printed sample post text. Re-running it needs your own API
    keys (Anthropic, YouTube, optionally Bluesky). Its result caches are keyed to
    exact prompt fingerprints (601ace4682 for extraction), so a re-run with a
    different prompt is a new experiment, not a replication.

data/posts_index_v4.csv          one row per collected post (28,422), no text:
    url, platform, source, cluster (video / thread / author / app),
    feature_tag (collector and query), first_pass_ai_related,
    verifier_category, verifier_keep, detected_language (Apple retained posts;
    langdetect), human_consensus_relevant (Y/N for the 728 hand-labeled posts),
    analyzed_final (the set behind Table 2).
data/extracted_aspect_sentiment_v4_all.csv       aspect pairs from the first
    pass (summary of at most 3 words + sentiment) for every AI-related post.
data/extracted_aspect_sentiment_v4_verified.csv  the same, restricted to posts
    retained by the verification pass.
data/verifier_verdicts_v4.csv    category assigned by the verification pass.

human_validation/usability_consensus_labels_v4.csv
    The 728 posts labeled by both authors (all 628 HarmonyOS first-pass

results/   theme_clusters_v4.csv, relevance_sensitivity_v4.csv,

WHY THERE IS NO RAW TEXT ("DEHYDRATED" RELEASE)
-------------------------------------------------
Posts are not redistributed: the YouTube API terms restrict redistribution of
stored comment content, and usernames raise privacy concerns. Every post is
identified by its public URL, so the text can be re-fetched ("rehydrated")
through the same official APIs, subject to deletions since collection.
Aspect summaries (at most three words, generated by the model) are released
because they are derived data, not user text.

HOW THE FINAL ESTIMATES WERE FORMED
-------------------------------------
HarmonyOS: all 628 candidate posts were hand-labeled, so its estimate uses only
the 302 posts both authors confirmed (first-pass aspect pairs of those posts).
Apple: posts retained by the verification pass and detected as English (9,003).
Statistic: positive share pos/(pos+neg) over aspect pairs; 95% CI by cluster
bootstrap (5,000 resamples of whole clusters, seed 0). Reportability rule,
declared before the final analysis: >= 30 clusters and no single cluster
> 20% of AI-related posts.

HUMAN LABELING RULEBOOK (as given to both raters)
---------------------------------------------------
Raters worked independently on blind copies (no model output shown), with
identical shuffled order, using drop-down menus. Disagreements were then
reconciled by discussion.

Relevant = Y when the post expresses an opinion, experience, complaint, praise,
or expectation about an AI FEATURE of the platform named for that row.
  Apple AI features: Apple Intelligence -- Writing Tools, Clean Up, Genmoji,
    Image Playground, notification/email summaries, Siri with Apple
    Intelligence, Visual Intelligence, Live Translation, ChatGPT integration in
    Siri, Private Cloud Compute.
  HarmonyOS AI features: Huawei AI on HarmonyOS devices -- Celia/Xiaoyi
    assistant and agents, Harmony Intelligence, AI camera features (pose,
    portrait, zoom, eraser, retouch), AI gestures, AI translation, other
    AI-driven HarmonyOS features.
  Also Y: comparisons that judge this platform's AI feature; hopes or
    disappointment about a feature; sarcasm judged by intended meaning.
Relevant = N when:
  - news, press release, announcement, ad, or tutorial with no personal opinion;
  - about the OS or phone in general (battery, design, apps, Google services,
    price, sanctions, geopolitics) without an AI-feature opinion;
  - about another company's AI, or the OTHER platform in the study;
  - about a third-party app;
  - LLM research or company news (model releases, benchmarks, controversies)
    unless the person judges using the feature on their device;
  - "Xiaoyi" or "Celia" used as a person's or character's name;
  - not in English (the study is English-only);
  - still unclear after reading the context (and link) -> N, noted "unclear".
Sentiment (only for Y): positive (overall favorable), negative (complaints,
  problems, disappointment, distrust), neutral (opinion present but neither),
  mixed (clearly both favorable and unfavorable points).

RESULTS OF THE VALIDATION (reproduced by the notebook)
--------------------------------------------------------
Inter-rater: relevance kappa 0.896 (94.8%); sentiment kappa 0.934 (96.3%).
Model vs consensus: relevance precision 0.653 (Apple 0.805, HarmonyOS 0.622),
recall 0.834, kappa 0.372; sentiment agreement 88.4%, kappa 0.797.
Nearly half of the HarmonyOS false positives (72 of 149) were non-English
Latin-script posts (mainly Indonesian) that passed the collection-time script
filter. The model-only HarmonyOS estimate (52.6%) is therefore superseded by the
human-validated 44.8%.
